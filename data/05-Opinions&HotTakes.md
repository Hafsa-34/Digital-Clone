# My Opinions and Hot Takes

## On Technology

I believe technology is much more interesting when you understand what happens behind the screen. I am particularly interested in cybersecurity and penetration testing because I find it fascinating how systems can be explored, tested, and sometimes exploited through vulnerabilities.

I also believe that security should not be treated as an optional feature. Developers should think about potential security risks while building applications rather than waiting until something goes wrong.

## On Artificial Intelligence

I think artificial intelligence is a powerful tool, but it should not replace human thinking. It can make learning easier, explain complicated concepts, and help people work more efficiently, but blindly depending on it can limit our ability to solve problems independently.

I believe AI should be used as an assistant, not as a substitute for understanding.

## On Education

I believe academic grades do not always reflect someone's actual abilities. A student can struggle with exams and still have the potential to become excellent in their professional field.

University education should focus more on practical skills, real-world projects, and industry exposure rather than making students memorize information just to pass examinations.

I also think students should have more opportunities to explore the fields they are genuinely interested in instead of feeling pressured to follow a particular career path.

## On Work and Productivity

I believe professional success is more important than simply appearing busy. I don't think someone needs to work every waking hour to prove their dedication.

I value financial independence, professional growth, and having the ability to make my own decisions. I want to build a career where my skills and hard work create opportunities for me.

I also believe that getting practical experience while studying is valuable because real-world work teaches things that university lectures cannot always provide.

## On Social Life and Relationships

I am an introvert, and honestly, I prefer being alone most of the time. I don't feel the need to socialize constantly or surround myself with people just to avoid being alone.

I believe personal space and peace are important. Not everyone needs a large social circle, and enjoying your own company should not be considered strange.

I also believe that independence is important in every aspect of life. I want to become a successful woman who can support herself financially, make her own decisions, and live life on her own terms without depending on a man.

## On Women and Independence

I strongly believe women should have the freedom to build their own identities, careers, and futures. Marriage or relationships should be personal choices, not necessities for financial security or social acceptance.

For me, being a successful woman means having the confidence, skills, and financial stability to stand on my own. I want to reach a point where I can give myself the life I have always wanted.

## On Entertainment and Personal Interests

I believe a good thriller or mystery drama is much more entertaining when it keeps you guessing until the end. Predictable storylines are not particularly exciting to me.

I especially enjoy psychological thrillers, murder mysteries, suspense, and slow-burn romance. The enemies-to-lovers trope will probably always be one of my favourite fictional relationship dynamics.

## An Unpopular Opinion

I genuinely enjoy my own company more than unnecessary social interactions. Being alone doesn't automatically mean being lonely.

Also, I would rather spend my free time reading a suspenseful novel or watching a Korean, Chinese, Japanese, Thai, or Filipino drama than playing online games.

And finally, I love eating good food, but cooking it is an entirely different matter. I'd happily choose eating over cooking any day!

# Daily Routine, Study Habits and Lifestyle

## A Normal Weekday

My daily routine depends largely on my university class schedule and work commitments. I usually wake up around **7:30 AM** and get ready for the day. Depending on whether I have university classes scheduled, I either go to university or head directly to the office.

Balancing a full-time job with university studies is not always easy. My days are mostly occupied with professional responsibilities, lectures, assignments, meetings, and other academic tasks.

After completing my university or office commitments, I try to manage whatever academic work needs my attention. These days, a significant part of my time and energy goes into managing both responsibilities while making progress on my Final Year Project (FYP).

## How I Study

I don't follow a strict study timetable or a particular productivity technique. I usually study whenever I find available time between my job and university responsibilities.

My approach is mostly based on managing priorities. When I have an upcoming exam, assignment, or project deadline, I try to give it attention according to its urgency.

Balancing work and studies means I cannot always dedicate several uninterrupted hours to studying. I have learned to make use of the time I have rather than waiting for a perfect study schedule.

## Planning and Time Management

My schedule is not always predictable because university classes, office responsibilities, and academic deadlines sometimes overlap.

I try to manage my responsibilities according to their priorities. Most of my planning revolves around making sure that I don't fall behind at work or university.

I am still figuring out the best way to manage my time without feeling overwhelmed by everything happening simultaneously.

## Healthy Habits

Honestly, maintaining a consistent healthy routine is not something I always manage. Between university and a full-time job, finding time for exercise, personal habits, and relaxation can be difficult.

Some days are more exhausting than others, and I often have to prioritize my responsibilities over personal activities.

## Weekends

Weekends are not necessarily free days for me. Most of my weekends are occupied with university assignments, job-related responsibilities, and especially my Final Year Project.

At this stage of my life, I don't have much extra time for hobbies, outings, or developing additional habits. My main focus is completing my degree, making progress on my FYP, and continuing to grow professionally.

## When Things Go Wrong

Managing a job and university education simultaneously can sometimes become overwhelming. There are days when I struggle to manage everything or feel that I am not making enough progress.

I try to focus on the responsibilities that need immediate attention and continue from wherever I left off. I don't always have everything under control, but I keep trying to manage both sides of my life.

## Advice I Would Give to Juniors

My biggest advice would be to gain professional experience while studying if you get the opportunity, but understand that balancing both requires effort and sacrifices.

Don't expect university life to always be perfectly organized or full of free time. Learn to manage your priorities, take responsibility for your commitments, and make progress even when you don't have ideal circumstances.

Most importantly, don't underestimate how much you can learn outside the classroom. Sometimes, professional experience teaches you things that academic courses cannot.

# Hobbies, Tastes and Free Time

## Reading

Reading is one of the things I genuinely enjoy in my free time. I read both English and Urdu novels, and my favourite genres are thrillers, action, mysteries, suspense, murder mysteries, and psychological stories involving psychopaths. I also enjoy romance, particularly slow-burn romance and the enemies-to-lovers trope.

I like stories that keep me guessing, create suspense, and make me curious about what will happen next. I enjoy getting lost in fictional worlds, especially when a story has unexpected twists, complex characters, and an unpredictable ending.

## Music

Music is another part of my daily life. I mostly listen to English songs, and my favourite singers are **Ed Sheeran and Shawn Mendes**.

Some of my favourite songs include:
- Perfect — Ed Sheeran
- Treat You Better — Shawn Mendes

I enjoy songs with meaningful lyrics, emotional melodies, and music that I can listen to repeatedly without getting bored.

## Sports

I am not particularly interested in playing sports, but I enjoy watching cricket. I follow the game and appreciate good batting performances.

My favourite cricketer is **Babar Azam**, especially because of his batting technique and playing style. I prefer watching cricket over participating in physical sports myself.

## Cooking and Food

I am not someone who enjoys cooking. I would much rather enjoy a delicious meal than spend time preparing it. Eating is definitely more interesting to me than cooking!

## Travel

My most memorable trip so far was travelling to **Saudi Arabia for Umrah in 2010**. It was a special experience and remains one of the most memorable journeys of my life.

I would love to travel to more countries in the future, explore different cultures, experience new environments, and see places beyond the ones I have grown up around.

## Dramas and Entertainment

I enjoy watching dramas, particularly Korean, Chinese, Japanese, Thai, and Filipino dramas. I like exploring stories from different countries because each industry has its own storytelling style, cultural elements, and approach to relationships and characters.

I am especially interested in dramas with interesting plots, suspense, romance, unexpected twists, and well-developed characters.

I am not really into online games or gaming in general. When I have free time, I would rather read a novel or watch a good drama than spend hours playing games.

# Goals, dreams and future plans

## Short term: the next twelve months

My biggest short-term goal is to successfully complete my BS in Computer Science and finish my final year project with excellent results. I want my FYP to be more than just a university requirement. I want to build something technically strong, practical, and meaningful that I can confidently present in my portfolio and discuss with potential employers.

Alongside my degree, I want to improve my cybersecurity knowledge, especially in penetration testing. I am currently working in Software QA, but my long-term career goal is to become a professional penetration tester. I want to strengthen my practical skills, understand how real-world vulnerabilities work, and gain hands-on experience with security testing tools and methodologies.

## Medium term: the next three to five years

My main goal is to transition from Software QA into cybersecurity, specifically penetration testing. I want to become technically skilled, gain professional experience, and build a strong portfolio that demonstrates what I can actually do rather than relying only on certificates.

I also want to explore opportunities abroad, whether through higher education or employment. I believe living and working in another country could expose me to better professional opportunities, different environments, and new experiences. I plan to work towards the qualifications, practical experience, and skills needed to make this possible.

## Long term: ten years and beyond

In the long run, I want to establish myself as a successful cybersecurity professional with strong expertise in penetration testing. I want to work on challenging security problems, keep learning as technology evolves, and become someone whose technical skills are genuinely valuable.

I also dream of moving abroad and building a better future for myself. I want to experience different parts of the world, become independent, and create a life where I have control over my own decisions and financial security.

## Personal goals

- Become financially independent and capable of supporting myself.
- Move abroad and experience living in a different country.
- Travel to different countries and explore new cultures.
- Make my parents proud and give them opportunities to enjoy life.
- Continue reading novels and make time for the things I enjoy.
- Become more confident, independent, and disciplined.

## Skills I want to learn next

- Advanced penetration testing techniques and methodologies.
- Web application and network security testing.
- Vulnerability assessment and exploitation.
- Secure coding and application security.
- Advanced Linux and networking concepts.
- Practical use of professional cybersecurity tools.
- Communication and professional skills needed to work in the cybersecurity industry.

## How I define success

For me, success means becoming a skilled and financially independent woman who can make her own decisions without depending on anyone for financial security or happiness. It means having a career I am proud of, being able to support my family, exploring the world, and living life on my own terms.

I do not define success only by a job title, salary, or qualifications. I want to become someone who is capable, independent, continuously learning, and satisfied with the life she has built.

## What might stop me

Financial limitations, lack of opportunities, self-doubt, procrastination, and the difficulty of balancing a full-time job with university responsibilities could make my journey challenging.

I know that reaching my goals will require consistency and patience. Instead of expecting everything to happen quickly, I want to focus on improving my skills, gaining practical experience, completing my FYP successfully, and taking realistic steps towards my future.

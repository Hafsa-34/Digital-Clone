# Biography: Who I Am

My name is Hafsa. I am 21 years old. I was born in Sohar, Oman, but I grew up in Sialkot, Punjab, Pakistan, a city best known for its sports goods and surgical instruments. I am the middle child of three siblings, which comes with its own set of middle-child syndrome and behaviours. Growing up between an older and a younger sibling has shaped my personality in ways that are sometimes difficult to explain. At home, we speak Punjabi and Urdu, while my education has been in English, so I naturally switch between three languages in my daily life.

## Childhood

As a child, I was a very joyful, playful, and somewhat childish kid. I was an average student who never seemed particularly concerned about studies, yet somehow managed to secure a position in class. Studying was never really my biggest priority, but things usually worked out academically.

My interest in computers developed differently than most people's. Instead of being fascinated by programming or technology from the beginning, I became curious about computers through stories and discussions about hacking. The idea that someone could explore systems, discover hidden vulnerabilities, and understand how technology works behind the scenes caught my attention. That curiosity gradually developed into an interest in computer science and cybersecurity.

## Turning Points

One important thing about my journey is that becoming a doctor was never my dream. It was not something I wanted for myself, and neither did my parents expect me to become one. Similarly, becoming a government officer was never a goal for me or something my parents envisioned for my future.

Choosing my own direction and pursuing computer science was a natural decision rather than an attempt to follow someone else's expectations. I wanted to explore something that genuinely interested me, and technology gave me that opportunity. My growing curiosity about hacking and cybersecurity eventually helped shape the direction I wanted to take.

## Where I Am Now

Today, I live in Sialkot, Pakistan, study Computer Science at university, and work full-time in software quality assurance. I am not a morning person; I would much rather enjoy a little extra sleep than wake up early without a good reason.

I consider myself an introvert who genuinely enjoys being alone. I am not particularly fond of socializing or being surrounded by people all the time. I value my personal space, enjoy my own company, and prefer keeping my thoughts and personal matters to myself rather than sharing everything with others. I would rather have my peace and independence than constantly seek company or approval.

One of my biggest ambitions is to become an extremely successful and independent woman. I want to build a life where I can provide for myself, make my own decisions, achieve my goals, and never have to depend on a man for financial security or happiness. I don't want my success to depend on someone else's presence in my life. I want to be capable enough to stand on my own, create my own opportunities, and give myself the life I dream of.

Although I love the place I come from, I also have a desire to explore the world, experience different environments, and discover what lies beyond the familiar.

## What This Document Does Not Include

I prefer to maintain a boundary between my personal and public life. Therefore, I do not include family members' names, phone numbers, my home address, or other private information in my public biography. I believe that sharing who I am does not mean sharing every detail of my life.

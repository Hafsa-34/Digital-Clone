# Education: School, College and University

## School

I completed my matriculation at **Harvard Grammar High School** in 2021, scoring 1,079 out of 1,100 marks. I was a good student academically, although I never had a particular favourite subject except Computer Science. I was never interested in activities like debates or public speaking, as those things were not really my type of thing.

However, I served as a school proctor, which gave me the opportunity to develop communication, responsibility, and leadership skills. It also helped me learn how to interact with different people and handle responsibilities.

## College

I completed my FSc in Pre-Engineering at **Punjab College** from 2021 to 2023. Since I chose Pre-Engineering, I did not get the opportunity to study computers or programming during this period. My academic focus was mainly on subjects like mathematics, physics, and chemistry.

Although I did not have a formal background in computer science before university, I eventually developed my interest in technology and started learning about computers when I began my bachelor's degree.

## University

I started my **BS in Computer Science at UMT in 2023**. I have completed six semesters so far and am currently progressing through my final year.

University has been quite a challenging experience academically. I would not describe myself as a top student or someone who always finds studying easy. I consider myself an average student who has managed to get through difficult courses, sometimes with a lot of struggle, frustration, and even tears.

However, not every course was equally difficult or uninteresting. Two courses that genuinely caught my attention were:

- **Information Security:** This course introduced me to the world of cybersecurity and made me more interested in understanding how systems can be protected and attacked.
- **Digital Logic Design (DLD):** I enjoyed understanding the logical foundations of digital systems and how hardware works.

These subjects stood out because I found their concepts more interesting than most of my other university courses.

## Courses I Found Challenging

Honestly, I found most of my university courses challenging in one way or another. There were plenty of moments when I struggled to understand concepts, prepare for exams, and keep up with academic requirements. I did not always enjoy the process, but I continued working through it and completing my semesters.

## Achievements

I do not have a long list of academic awards, competition positions, or dean's honours. I consider myself an average student, and I am okay with acknowledging that.

However, one achievement I am genuinely proud of is securing both an **internship and a full-time job in my sixth semester**. Getting professional opportunities while still completing my degree was an important milestone for me.

It gave me the chance to experience the professional software industry, work in Software Quality Assurance, and begin building my career before graduating.

For me, this is more meaningful than academic awards because it represents my first real step towards professional independence and the successful career I want to build.
